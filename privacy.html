Privacy policy.

Anime.TV is built to be read, watched and left alone. There is no account, no feed and no server-side profile of you. This page explains the small amount of data that does move.

01 / Collection

Information we collect

We believe in keeping your browsing experience as private as possible. Anime.TV does not require you to create an account, and we do not store your personal information on our servers.

02 / On this device

Local storage (your history)

To provide features like “Continue Watching” and your “Watch History”, we use your browser's local storage. Your viewing history is saved directly on your own device, not on our servers. Clearing your browser data will erase this history.

03 / Outside services

Third-party services

We use third-party APIs (such as Jikan/MyAnimeList and TMDB) to fetch anime metadata, descriptions and cover art. We also use third-party form providers (such as Formspree) to handle the “Report a Bug” feature. When interacting with these features, basic network data (like IP addresses) may be visible to these third parties in accordance with their own privacy policies.

04 / Updates

Changes to this policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes to this Privacy Policy are effective when they are posted on this page.
